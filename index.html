Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking home services on Mr.Helper.

What is Mr.Helper? +

Mr.Helper is India's trusted home services marketplace app that connects you with KYC-verified local service providers for plumbing, electrical work, AC repair, cleaning, painting, carpentry, and more. We focus on trust, location-based matching, and provider verification to ensure you receive safe, reliable service every time.

How do I book a service on Mr.Helper? +

Download the Mr.Helper app from the Google Play Store, create your account, select the service you need (plumber, electrician, AC repair, etc.), enter your location, and submit a service request. A verified provider near you will accept and arrive at your doorstep.

Are Mr.Helper service providers verified? +

Yes, every service provider on Mr.Helper undergoes a strict KYC verification and background check process before being approved on the platform. We verify identity documents, address proof, and professional skills to ensure only trusted professionals serve you.

Which cities does Mr.Helper serve? +

Mr.Helper currently serves Warangal, Hyderabad, Kazipet and Hanamkonda in Telangana — covering areas like Subedari, Mulugu Road, Hunter Road, Nakkalagutta, and more. We are expanding to Bangalore, Chennai, and other major Indian cities.

How much do home services cost on Mr.Helper? +

Mr.Helper offers transparent pricing with no hidden charges. Service costs vary by type — plumbing starts from ₹199, electrical from ₹149, AC repair from ₹299, and cleaning from ₹499. You see the price before you book.

Is Mr.Helper available on iPhone (iOS)? +

Currently, Mr.Helper is available on Android via the Google Play Store. Our iOS app is under development and will be available soon. Stay tuned for updates!

  • Microphone (Audio): We access your device's microphone only when you use the voice search feature to find services. Audio data is processed locally or sent temporarily to speech recognition servers and is not permanently stored.
  • Photos and Media: We access your photo gallery only when you choose to upload a profile picture or service images.

  • To send you transactional notifications and promotional offers (if opted in).

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • With Service Providers: Your location and contact details may be shared with the specific provider you book to enable them to fulfill the service.

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

You can access, update, or delete your account information at any time within the app settings. You may also revoke permissions (Location, Microphone, etc.) through your device settings, though this may limit app functionality.
