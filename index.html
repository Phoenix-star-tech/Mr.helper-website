Frequently Asked Questions

What is Mr.Helper? +

Mr.Helper is India's trusted home services marketplace app connecting households with KYC-verified local service providers for plumbing, electrical work, AC repair, cleaning, painting, carpentry, transport and tuition.

How do I book a service on Mr.Helper? +

Download the Mr.Helper app from Google Play, select the service you need, choose your city/locality in Telangana, and submit your booking. A nearby verified technician is assigned and arrives at your doorstep.

Are Mr.Helper service providers verified? +

Yes! Every single service partner undergoes thorough KYC verification (Aadhaar, address verification, and trade skill testing) before approval.

Which cities does Mr.Helper serve? +

We currently operate across Warangal, Hyderabad, Kazipet and Hanamkonda covering localities like Subedari, Nakkalagutta, Hunter Road, Hitec City, Gachibowli, Kukatpally, and Mulugu Road.

How much do home services cost on Mr.Helper? +

Mr.Helper offers standard, transparent pricing: electrical starting from ₹149, plumbing from ₹199, AC repair from ₹299, and cleaning from ₹499. You see the price in the app before booking.

Is Mr.Helper available on iPhone (iOS)? +

Currently Mr.Helper is available for all Android devices via Google Play Store. The iOS app is actively under development.

Terms of Service

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Please read these Terms of Service carefully before using the Mr.Helper mobile application and platform operated under Vashik Technologies.

1. Description of Marketplace

Mr.Helper is an on-demand hyper-local marketplace connecting consumers with independent third-party service providers. We facilitate discovery, dispatch, KYC verification, and rate-card transparency.

2. Rate Cards & Fair Billing

Base inspection and service rates are shown before booking. Any additional spare parts or extra labor are quoted and confirmed with the homeowner prior to work commencement.

3. User Conduct

Homeowners must provide a safe working environment for service professionals. Fraudulent or abusive activity may result in suspension.
