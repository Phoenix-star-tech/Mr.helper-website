Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

At Mr.Helper, operated under Vashik Technologies, we prioritize transparency and privacy. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, and safeguard personal information when you use our mobile application and services.

  • Location Data: To match you with nearby KYC-verified plumbers, electricians, or cleaners, we collect precise or approximate geolocation.
  • Contact & Account Info: Name, phone number, and email address used for booking confirmation and customer verification.
  • Device & Usage Details: App crash analytics, device model, and OS version to optimize performance.

2. How We Use Information

  • To dispatch verified service professionals directly to your address within 15-30 minutes.

3. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell personal information to third parties. Verified technicians only receive location and contact details necessary to fulfill the requested home service.
