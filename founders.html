Executive Leadership

Meet the Visionary Founders of Mr. Helper

Founded under Vashik Technologies, Mr. Helper is reimagining urban living and grassroots skilled trades in India with technology, fairness, and speed within 24 hours.

Why We Built Mr. Helper

In urban and developing hubs like Hyderabad, Warangal, and Hanamkonda, finding an honest, skilled plumber or electrician in an emergency used to be a frustrating guessing game. Homeowners had to ask neighbours, haggle over arbitrary prices, and wait hours for someone who might not even show up.

At the same time, thousands of talented local tradesmen—electricians, plumbers, AC technicians, carpenters, painters, and salon professionals—struggled to find steady, dignified work near their homes. They relied on local tea stalls or contractor middlemen who took heavy commissions.

Mr. Helper was created by our founding team to bridge this exact divide. By pairing a seamless Android application with strict background verification, standardized transparent rate cards, and a reliable dispatch engine within 24 hours, we empower both the homeowner and the technician.
